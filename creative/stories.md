# Story & World-Building

The Storycrafting Gamer treats narrative as a system that works alongside gameplay.

## Story pillars

- Characters with clear wants and conflicts
- Worlds with internal rules
- Choices with consequences
- Mysteries that reward curiosity
- Lore that supports rather than overwhelms gameplay

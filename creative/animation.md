# Animation

Animation content focuses on visual storytelling, character motion, cinematics, and the relationship between animation and gameplay.

## Topics

- Character animation
- Cinematic blocking
- Facial expressions
- Creature design
- Environmental motion
- Storyboarding
- Game cinematics
- Animation pipelines

## Creative principle

**Every movement should communicate something.**

A character's pose, timing, expression, and movement can reveal personality or advance a story without dialogue.

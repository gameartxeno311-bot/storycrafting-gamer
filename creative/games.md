# Game Concepts

## Current direction

Develop original game concepts that combine memorable gameplay with strong narrative hooks.

## Concept template

### Title
Working title

### Genre
Genre and gameplay perspective

### Core hook
One sentence explaining what makes the game interesting.

### Player fantasy
What the player gets to feel like they are doing.

### Core loop
1. Explore
2. Discover
3. Make a meaningful choice
4. Overcome a challenge
5. Unlock a new possibility

### Narrative hook
The central story question.

### Visual identity
Art direction, environments, characters, and mood.

### Prototype goal
The smallest playable version that proves the core idea is fun.

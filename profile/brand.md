# Brand Guide

## Header

**The Storycrafting Gamer**

## Tagline

**Create a game people love.**

## Brand themes

**Games · Stories · Animation · Creation**

## Visual direction

- Cinematic gaming atmosphere
- Cozy creator workspace
- Dark hoodie / casual creator aesthetic
- Warm practical lighting with cool gaming-screen accents
- Sketches, books, controllers, monitors, and development props
- Modern creator/streamer presentation

## Emotional tone

Creative, welcoming, ambitious, curious, and optimistic.

## Avatar direction

A realistic-looking young male creator with tousled dark hair, a relaxed expression, and a dark hoodie in a cozy game-development workspace.

## Banner direction

A wide cinematic creator-studio scene with the creator alongside gaming equipment, story notes, books, animation references, and a fantasy game-development atmosphere.
